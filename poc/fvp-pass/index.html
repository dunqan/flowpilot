    Scanning

    ↓ jNo, next item

    eFix in place (typos)

    w sWaiting / someday (leaves the list)

    EscLeave working mode without changing anything

    typeReplace the item
    TabPull the original in to tweak it (phone: tap “Use original”)
    Enter EscSave; with nothing typed, the original is kept
